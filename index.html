About Us

Lacta Trade Company s.r.o. is a Czech company specializing in case-in trading. We conduct sales and delivery of Technical Acid Casein and are constantly searching for new partners for long-term business cooperation for mutual benefit.

Our prices are based on current stock exchange rates at the time of purchase and are negotiable depending on the required quantity.

Product Information

  • Fat content: max 1.5%
  • Acidity: max 50%
  • Humidity: max 12%

Packaging: 25 kg paper sacks with polyethylene liner.
Country of origin: Ukraine.
Quantity and delivery terms are defined by contract.
Price terms: CPT – Incoterms 2010.
